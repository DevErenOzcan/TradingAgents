## Technical Indicator Selection & Suitability
To analyze TSM’s market condition without redundancy, we have selected **8 complementary indicators** from the standard toolkit. These indicators are tailored to a highly volatile, liquid, and trend-following tech stock like TSM:

1. **close_200_sma (Long-Term Trend Benchmark):** Establishes the macro-trend direction. It filters out short-term market noise and identifies ultimate support zones during major market corrections.
2. **close_50_sma (Medium-Term Trend):** Serves as a dynamic benchmark for medium-term trend direction and identifies major overhead resistance levels during counter-trend consolidations.
3. **close_10_ema (Responsive Short-Term Average):** Captures immediate shifts in price direction and provides precise entries by tracking short-term trend reversals.
4. **macd (Trend Momentum):** Tracks the acceleration or deceleration of price momentum by calculating the difference between two moving averages.
5. **macds (MACD Signal Line):** An EMA smoothing of the MACD line, used to identify high-probability trend crossovers.
6. **rsi (Relative Strength Index):** Evaluates overbought/oversold conditions on a 0–100 scale, helping to spot trend exhaustion and reversal zones.
7. **boll (Bollinger Middle Band):** A 20-period simple moving average that acts as the baseline for volatility bands and defines the short-term directional bias.
8. **atr (Average True Range):** Measures market volatility over a 14-period lookback, crucial for setting volatility-adjusted stop-losses and position-sizing parameters.
