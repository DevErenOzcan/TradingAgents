## 1. Executive Summary
Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSM) continues to cement its status as the bedrock of the global technology and semiconductor sector. As of August 10, 2026, TSM boasts a market capitalization of **$2.18 Trillion USD**, reflecting its monopoly-like dominance in advanced node manufacturing (3nm, 2nm) and packaging technologies (CoWoS) critical for the artificial intelligence (AI) revolution.

A rigorous evaluation of TSM’s financial performance over the past week and historical trends reveals a company experiencing unprecedented revenue acceleration, margin expansion, and cash flow generation. TSM's trailing twelve months (TTM) revenue stands at **NT$4.44 Trillion** (approx. **$138.8 Billion USD**), with a net profit margin of **49.92%**, resulting in TTM Net Income of **NT$2.22 Trillion** (approx. **$69.3 Billion USD**). 

With a remarkably low PEG ratio of **1.01** and a Forward P/E of **19.44** (supported by a Forward EPS estimate of **$21.61 USD**), TSM represents a highly compelling, growth-at-a-reasonable-price (GARP) opportunity.

## 2. Company Profile & Strategic Moat
TSM operates as the world’s largest dedicated semiconductor foundry. The company’s primary moat lies in its **Process Technology Leadership** and **Scale**:
*   **Advanced Nodes Dominance:** TSM is the sole or dominant producer of the world's most advanced chips (3nm and the upcoming 2nm node) for industry giants such as NVIDIA, Apple, AMD, Qualcomm, and Broadcom. 
*   **AI Tailwind:** The exponential demand for AI accelerators and high-performance computing (HPC) has led to a supply-demand mismatch where TSM holds extreme pricing power.
*   **Geographical Diversification:** TSM is actively mitigating geopolitical risks by building and expanding advanced fabs in Arizona (USA), Kumamoto (Japan), and Dresden (Germany), backed by massive government subsidies.

TSM’s top-line and bottom-line growth trajectories have significantly accelerated over the last four years, driven by the structural AI boom.

#### 1. Annual Revenue and Net Income Trends (NT$):
*   **FY 2022:** Revenue: NT$2.26T | Net Income: NT$992.9B

*   **FY 2024:** Revenue: NT$2.89T (+33.9%) | Net Income: NT$1.16T (+36.0%) — *Strong cyclical recovery and early AI chip demand.*
*   **FY 2025:** Revenue: NT$3.81T (+31.6%) | Net Income: NT$1.70T (+46.5%) — *Full-scale AI acceleration.*
*   **TTM (Q3 2025 – Q2 2026):** Revenue: NT$4.44T (+16.5% vs FY25) | Net Income: NT$2.22T (+30.5% vs FY25) — *Growth continues to break records.*

#### 2. Quarterly Acceleration (NT$):
*   **Q3 2025:** Revenue: NT$989.9B | Net Income: NT$452.3B
*   **Q4 2025:** Revenue: NT$1,046.1B | Net Income: NT$485.5B

#### 3. Phenomenal Margin Expansion:
TSM’s profitability metrics have reached historic levels, demonstrating strong operating leverage:
*   **Gross Margin Trend:** Rose from **54.36%** in FY23, to **59.89%** in FY25, and reached **67.72%** in Q2 2026. This expansion reflects TSM’s success in passing higher wafer pricing (especially for 3nm/5nm) onto customers.
*   **Operating Margin Trend:** Reached **60.34%** in Q2 2026 (TTM: 56.10%), up from 42.62% in FY23.
*   **Net Profit Margin (TTM):** Stands at **49.92%** (reaching **55.62%** in Q2 2026).

TSM maintains one of the most fortressed balance sheets in the entire technology ecosystem, providing it with unmatched defensive durability.

*   **Enormous Cash Reserves:** As of June 30, 2026, TSM holds **NT$3.52 Trillion** (approx. **$109.9 Billion USD**) in Cash, Cash Equivalents, and Short-Term Investments.
*   **Low Debt Profile:** Total Debt stands at **NT$982.4 Billion** (approx. **$30.7 Billion USD**).
*   **Massive Net Cash Position:** Net Cash = NT$2.54 Trillion (approx. **$79.2 Billion USD**). 
*   **Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** An exceptionally low **15.17%**, indicating negligible credit risk.
*   **Liquidity:** Current Ratio of **2.458** ensures that short-term obligations are easily covered.
*   **Return on Capital:** TSM delivers an extraordinary Return on Equity (**ROE**) of **39.97%** and Return on Assets (**ROA**) of **19.01%**, proving highly efficient asset utilization and capital management.

### C. Cash Flow Dynamics & Capital Allocation
TSM is a cash-generating powerhouse that effectively balances heavy capital reinvestment with robust shareholder returns.

*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Generated **NT$2.27 Trillion** in FY2025. In the first half of 2026 alone, TSM generated **NT$1.48 Trillion** in operating cash flow.
*   **Aggressive Capital Expenditures (CapEx):** TSM’s TTM CapEx (Purchase of PPE) reached **NT$1.49 Trillion** (approx. **$46.6 Billion USD**). This aggressive investment is targeted at expanding N3/N2 capacity and CoWoS packaging facilities globally to secure market dominance.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF) Generation:** Despite massive CapEx, TSM generated a TTM FCF of **NT$1.14 Trillion** (approx. **$35.5 Billion USD**).
*   **Dividend Growth:** TSM paid NT$531.6 Billion in dividends over the TTM period. At current valuations, this translates to a **0.9% dividend yield**, which is highly sustainable given the low payout ratio relative to cash flow (payout ratio is ~46.8% of FCF).
