### 1. Business Overview & Secular Catalysts
TTMI is a leading global manufacturer of technology-enabled printed circuit boards (PCBs), backplanes, and radio frequency (RF) components. It is one of the few large-scale Western players with a significant domestic manufacturing footprint. This positions the company perfectly to capture two major secular growth drivers:

1. **AI Infrastructure & HPC Acceleration**: Modern AI GPUs and server architectures require ultra-high-layer-count, highly advanced PCBs with extreme signal integrity and low-loss substrates. TTMI is key in supplying these specialized components, experiencing massive demand as hyper-scalers build out data centers.
2. **Aerospace & Defense (A&D) Reshoring**: Geopolitical tensions have intensified demand for secure, domestic microelectronics. With significant US-based capacity, TTMI is a prime beneficiary of increased US Department of Defense spending and funding from the CHIPS Act.

### 3. Balance Sheet Strength & Leverage Analysis
TTMI maintains a highly resilient, liquid balance sheet capable of supporting aggressive physical expansion:

* **Liquidity:** Current assets stand at $1.91B as of March 31, 2026, against current liabilities of $1.02B. This yields a strong **Current Ratio of 1.885** and **Working Capital of $898.31M**.
* **Capital Base Growth:** Net Property, Plant & Equipment (PPE) expanded from $948.21M at the end of 2024 to **$1.17B** as of Q1 2026, demonstrating huge physical capacity deployment.
* **Conservative Leverage:** Total debt is $1.05B (consisting mainly of $911.84M long-term debt and capital lease obligations). The **Debt-to-Equity ratio is very manageable at 57.1%**, particularly given TTM EBITDA of $438.90M.

### 4. Cash Flow Dynamics: "Good" vs "Bad" Cash Burn
Traders might initially view the **TTM Free Cash Flow of -$55.0M** (-$55,001,000) as a risk. However, deep-dive analysis reveals this is highly constructive cash consumption:

1. **Massive Capital Expenditures (CapEx):** TTMI spent $292.57M in CapEx in 2025 and a massive **$106.85M in Q1 2026 alone** (TTM CapEx is over $336M). This cash is being used to build out state-of-the-art facilities in the US and globally to fulfill contracted demand in AI and Defense.
2. **Working Capital Build-up:** Due to the 30%+ YoY revenue growth, TTMI experienced a -$96.35M working capital adjustment in Q1 2026 (primarily growth in accounts receivable of -$54.34M and raw materials of -$30.15M).
3. **Core Cash Generation:** Operating Cash Flow remains very strong, coming in at $291.88M in 2025.

**Verdict:** This is "growth-oriented" cash consumption rather than structural cash burning. TTMI is aggressively investing to lock in long-term market dominance.

* **Deeply Undervalued PEG:** With a **PEG ratio of 0.36**, the stock is exceptionally cheap relative to its earnings growth rate. Any PEG below 1.0 is considered undervalued; a PEG of 0.36 is a rare finding in the high-demand AI hardware supply chain.
