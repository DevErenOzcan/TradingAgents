### Technical Indicator Selection & Rationale

To analyze TTMI’s current volatile market conditions, we have selected **8 highly complementary technical indicators** to provide a multi-dimensional view of trend, momentum, volatility, and volume-weighted structures:

1. **Close 10-day EMA (Responsive Short-Term Trend)**: Captures immediate price direction and helps identify early trigger points for short-term trend reversals.
2. **Close 50-day SMA (Medium-Term Trend)**: Serves as the primary overhead trend barrier and dynamic resistance; crucial for identifying when the medium-term downtrend is formally broken.
3. **Close 200-day SMA (Long-Term Trend Benchmark)**: The ultimate line of defense for the long-term macro trend. Used to verify structural support during deep corrections.
4. **Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD)**: Evaluates structural momentum shifts and absolute trend strength.
5. **MACD Histogram (macdh)**: Measures the rate of change in momentum, providing early warning signs of trend exhaustion and bullish/bearish crossovers.
6. **Relative Strength Index (RSI)**: Identifies oversold capitulation zones (<30) and tracks the strength of the subsequent recovery.
7. **Volume-Weighted Moving Average (VWMA - 20-day)**: Blends price action with volume to determine where institutional "smart money" is transacting, filtering out low-volume retail noise.
8. **Average True Range (ATR - 14-day)**: Measures the current trading range and absolute market volatility, which is vital for setting volatility-adjusted stop-losses and position sizes.
