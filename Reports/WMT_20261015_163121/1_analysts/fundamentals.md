## Executive Summary & Overview

Walmart Inc. (WMT) continues to perform as a premier global omni-channel retailer, capitalizing on its scale, supply chain automation, and high-margin revenue drivers (such as advertising via Walmart Connect, marketplace fees, and e-commerce fulfillment services). 

As of October 15, 2026, Walmart’s market capitalization stands at **$921.58 Billion**, reinforcing its leadership in the Consumer Defensive sector. Key financial highlights include TTM revenues of **$735.84 Billion**, TTM gross profit of **$182.76 Billion**, and TTM net income of **$22.08 Billion**. 

Despite trading below its 200-day moving average ($118.20) and 52-week high ($135.16), Walmart demonstrates strong operating income expansion (+28.78% YoY in Q2 FY2026), driven by gross margin gains and disciplined cost management. However, its premium valuation (TTM P/E of 42.09x and Forward P/E of 36.03x) requires sustained earnings execution.

## 2. Earnings & Income Statement Dynamics

Walmart's quarterly performance highlights significant operational leverage, as revenue growth flows efficiently down to operating and net income lines.

### Quarterly Revenue & Operating Performance Trajectory:
* **Q2 FY2026 (Ended 2026-07-31):**
  * **Total Revenue:** $187.94B (+5.94% YoY compared to $177.40B in Q2 FY2025)
  * **Gross Profit:** $49.13B (+10.08% YoY) — Gross margin improved from 25.16% to 26.14%
  * **Operating Income:** $9.38B (+28.78% YoY compared to $7.29B in Q2 FY2025) — Operating margin expanded to 4.99%
  * **Net Income:** $6.37B ($0.80 Diluted EPS)
* **Q1 FY2026 (Ended 2026-04-30):**
  * **Total Revenue:** $177.75B | **Gross Profit:** $44.69B | **Operating Income:** $7.49B | **Net Income:** $5.33B ($0.67 EPS)
* **Q4 FY2025 (Ended 2026-01-31):**
  * **Total Revenue:** $190.66B | **Gross Profit:** $47.04B | **Operating Income:** $8.71B | **Net Income:** $4.24B ($0.53 EPS)
* **Q3 FY2025 (Ended 2025-10-31):**
  * **Total Revenue:** $179.50B | **Gross Profit:** $44.79B | **Operating Income:** $6.70B | **Net Income:** $6.14B ($0.77 EPS)

### Key Earnings Insights:
1. **Margin Expansion Drivers:** Gross profit grew nearly twice as fast as sales (+10.08% vs +5.94% YoY in Q2 2026), benefiting from lower supply chain disruptions, marketplace growth, and high-margin advertising revenue.
2. **Operating Leverage:** Operating expense growth was controlled at $39.75B in Q2 2026, allowing operating margins to widen to nearly 5.0%, a notable achievement for discount retail at Walmart's scale.

## 4. Cash Flow Efficiency & Reinvestment Strategy

Walmart generates substantial cash flow, supporting reinvestment into tech/automation while returning capital to shareholders:

* **Operating Cash Flow:** Generated **$14.97 Billion** in Q2 FY2026, up from $4.74B in Q1 FY2026 and $12.94B in Q2 FY2025.
* **Capital Expenditures (CapEx):** Heavy investment rate of **$7.50 Billion** in Q2 FY2026 (~$29.4B TTM CapEx), targeting automated fulfillment centers, store remodels, and digital capabilities.
* **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Achieved **$7.48 Billion** in Q2 FY2026 (TTM FCF stands at **$7.13 Billion** after absorbing multi-year peak CapEx cycles).
* **Capital Return Program:**
  * **Share Repurchases:** Accelerated to **$3.02 Billion** in Q2 FY2026 (up from $2.08B in Q1 FY2026 and $1.65B in Q2 FY2025). Diluted average shares dropped from 8.016B in Q2 2025 to 7.942B in Q2 2026.
  * **Dividend Payments:** Disbursed **$1.97 Billion** in Q2 FY2026 (~$7.70B annualized).
