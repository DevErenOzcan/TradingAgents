### Technical Trend Analysis

During this period, the price consistently traded above its 10 EMA, and volatility peaked, as evidenced by an ATR of **9.58**.

On **2026-07-29**, when the stock closed at its low of **47.34**, the RSI bottomed at **32.95**—the lowest reading of the entire period, signaling extreme near-term oversold conditions.
