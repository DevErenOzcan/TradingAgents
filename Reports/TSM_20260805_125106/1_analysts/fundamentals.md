## 6. Valuation & Actionable "Database Mismatch" Arbitrage
Standard financial screens display a **Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio of 87.0** for TSM, which often deters value-conscious investors. However, an analysis of the underlying share data reveals a **significant database calculation error**:

### The P/B Discrepancy Explained:
1.  **1 TSM ADR represents 5 Ordinary Shares** traded on the Taiwan Stock Exchange.
2.  TSM's total book value of equity is **NT$6,432,518,000,000**, with **25,932,370,067 ordinary shares** outstanding.
3.  This translates to a book value of **NT$248.05 per ordinary share**, which at standard conversion rates is roughly **$4.80 USD** (matching the database's reported Book Value of $4.795).
4.  Standard financial databases have erroneously divided the **ADR price ($417.20 - $425.42 USD)** by the **ordinary share book value ($4.795 USD)**, yielding the artificial **87.0 P/B ratio** ($417.20 / $4.795 = 87.0).
5.  **The Correct P/B Calculation:** Because 1 ADR represents 5 ordinary shares, the book value per ADR is $4.795 * 5 = **$23.975 USD**. Therefore, the actual Price-to-Book ratio is **17.4** (or **10.76** if using the current nominal exchange rate of 32 TWD/USD). 

This means TSM is **significantly cheaper** relative to its assets than standard screens report, representing a major arbitrage insight for professional traders.
