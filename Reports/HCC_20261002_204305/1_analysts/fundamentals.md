## 2. Business Profile & Macro Environment

### Metallurgical Coal vs. Thermal Coal
Traders must distinguish Warrior Met Coal from traditional thermal coal miners. Thermal coal is burned for electricity generation and faces severe, long-term secular headwinds from renewable energy and natural gas. 

In contrast, **metallurgical (coking) coal** is a critical metallurgical agent used in blast furnaces to smelt iron ore into steel. There are currently no commercially viable, large-scale substitute technologies for coking coal in primary steelmaking. 

### Macro Drivers: Infrastructure and Indian Demand
The demand side for premium coking coal remains robust, driven by global infrastructure development and industrialization. Specifically, **India** is experiencing a massive, multi-decade expansion in steel production capacity. Because India has very limited domestic reserves of high-quality coking coal, it relies heavily on imports. 

HCC’s strategic location on the US Gulf Coast allows it to ship highly competitive, premium low-volatile and mid-volatile coking coal to European and Asian markets (especially India, Japan, and South Korea) via the Port of Mobile, Alabama. This geographic advantage insulates HCC from domestic US rail bottlenecks and positions it as a key beneficiary of the global steel super-cycle.

  * As a result, **Free Cash Flow skyrocketed to +$103.35M in Q2 2026 alone**.
* **The Outlook for Cash Flow:** As Blue Creek shifts from development to commercial production, CapEx will continue to normalize toward maintenance levels, while coal production volume will scale by up to **60%** (adding ~4.8 million short tons per year of high-margin capacity). This combination is set to make HCC a massive, high-yield free cash flow machine in late 2026 and 2027.
