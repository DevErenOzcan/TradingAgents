### Indicator Selection Rationales

To conduct a robust and non-redundant analysis of RXRX, we have selected seven complementary technical indicators spanning four critical categories:

   * *Rationale*: Captures rapid momentum shifts. Crucial for verifying whether the current two-day bounce has immediate follow-through or represents a temporary blip.
2. **Medium-Term Trend Hurdle**: **50-day SMA (`close_50_sma`)**
   * *Rationale*: Serves as a dynamic pivot point. Identifying where the stock stands relative to this average helps determine if the recovery is transitioning into a broader medium-term trend.
3. **Long-Term Trend Anchor**: **200-day SMA (`close_200_sma`)**
   * *Rationale*: Establishes the macro regime. RXRX is historically volatile; referencing the 200-day SMA prevents us from misinterpreting a tactical swing as a full macro bull market.
4. **Trend-Following Momentum**: **MACD (`macd`) & Signal Line (`macds`) / Histogram (`macdh`)**
   * *Rationale*: Helps identify momentum transitions. The MACD histogram's movement out of deep negative territory serves as an early-stage trend reversal signal.
5. **Overbought/Oversold Oscillator**: **RSI (`rsi`)**
   * *Rationale*: Quantifies buyer vs. seller strength. Moving averages can lag, but RSI shows whether the stock is overextended or has ample room to run.
6. **Volatility Boundaries**: **Bollinger Bands (`boll`, `boll_ub`, `boll_lb`)**
   * *Rationale*: Defines the statistically expected trading envelope. The relationship between the price and the middle band (20-day SMA) helps confirm breakout validation.
7. **Absolute Volatility**: **Average True Range (`atr`)**
   * *Rationale*: Essential for risk management. Tracks the average daily trading range to determine volatility-adjusted stop-loss placements.
