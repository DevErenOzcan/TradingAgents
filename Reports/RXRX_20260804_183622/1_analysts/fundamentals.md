* **Supercomputing Infrastructure**: In collaboration with **NVIDIA** (which made a $50 million equity investment in Recursion), the company built **BioHive-1** and **BioHive-2**, which are among the most powerful supercomputers in the biotechnology sector. These supercomputers train proprietary foundational models to predict drug-target interactions.
* **Strategic Alliances**: 
  * **Roche and Genentech**: A collaboration of up to 40 programs in neuroscience and oncology, carrying potential milestone payments exceeding $11 billion.
  * **Bayer**: Focused on fibrotic diseases, providing upfront capital and milestone payments.
  * **NVIDIA**: Developing and licensing foundation models for commercial biology and chemistry applications.

Recursion’s balance sheet as of March 31, 2026, is exceptionally strong, presenting minimal solvency or liquidity risks.
* **Robust Liquidity**: Current assets stand at **$709.56 million**, including **$654.47 million** in cash and cash equivalents. With current liabilities at **$129.70 million**, Recursion boasts an outstanding **Current Ratio of 5.47x**.
* **Excellent Capital Structure**: Total debt is very low at **$72.38 million** (primarily consisting of long-term capital leases for laboratory spaces and supercomputing equipment), representing a **Debt-to-Equity ratio of just 7.06%**.

Crucially, **$147.51 million** of this total (47%) consists of deferred revenue ($114.72M non-current and $32.79M current). * **Asset Value Backing**: Total stockholders' equity is **$1.02 billion**. With a book value per share of **$1.93**, the stock trades at a **Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio of only 1.70x**, which is extremely low for a high-growth, high-tech biotechnology platform.

### **3. Income Statement & Expense Control Trends**
While annual revenue has grown steadily, the major takeaway is the dramatic and consistent improvement in quarterly cost discipline.

* **Revenue Growth & Lumpiness**: 
  * Annual revenue grew from **$39.68 million in 2022** to **$74.26 million in 2025** (a 23% CAGR).
  * Quarterly revenue is highly lumpy, driven by partnership milestones. Q4 2025 revenue peaked at **$35.35 million**, while Q1 2026 revenue fell to **$6.30 million** (down from $14.82 million in Q1 2025). This lumpiness is typical of clinical-stage biotech and should not alarm long-term investors.
* **The Pivotal Cost Reduction Trend**: 
  Recursion has aggressively rationalized its cost structure over the past year:
  * **Total Quarterly Expenses**: Decreased from **$206.19 million** (Q1 2025) $\rightarrow$ **$195.33 million** (Q2 2025) $\rightarrow$ **$177.19 million** (Q3 2025) $\rightarrow$ **$143.69 million** (Q4 2025) $\rightarrow$ **$134.81 million** (Q1 2026). This represents a **34.6% reduction in total quarterly expenses** YoY.
  * **R&D Expense Decline**: Trimmed from **$129.63 million** in Q1 2025 to **$87.90 million** in Q1 2026 (a 32.2% reduction) as the platform matured and heavy upfront computing infrastructure investments wound down.
  * **G&A Expense Decline**: Trimmed from **$54.65 million** in Q1 2025 to **$34.59 million** in Q1 2026 (a 36.7% reduction).
  * **Shrinking Net Losses**: As a result of these cost-cutting measures, quarterly Net Losses shrank from **-$202.49 million** (Q1 2025) to **-$117.50 million** (Q1 2026), a massive **42.0% reduction in net losses**.

* **Dilution Deceleration**: In the most recent quarter (Q1 2026), share issuance came to a virtual halt, with shares increasing by only 0.46% to **530.63 million**.
* **Cash Burn and Runway Calculation**:
  * **TTM Operating Cash Burn**: -$320.95 million.

  * **TTM Free Cash Flow (FCF) Burn**: -$328.08 million.
  * **Q1 2026 Annualized FCF Burn**: -$81.44 million $\times$ 4 quarters = -$325.76 million.
  * **Cash Runway**: With **$654.47 million** in cash and cash equivalents and an annual FCF burn rate of **~$328 million**, Recursion has **exactly 2.0 years (24 months) of cash runway** (funding operations through mid-2028). This provides an immense runway to achieve clinical readouts without needing immediate dilutive capital raises.
