### 2. The Business Transformation: Pure-Play Aerospace
To appreciate GE's financial history, traders must note the dramatic reduction in Total Assets from **$188.85 Billion** in 2022 to **$127.67 Billion** in Q2 2026. This contraction is not a sign of operational decay but rather the execution of one of the most successful corporate spin-offs in history:
1. **GE HealthCare (GEHC)** was spun off in early 2023.
2. **GE Vernova (GEV)**, the power and energy business, was spun off in early 2024.

Today, GE Aerospace is a highly streamlined, asset-light enterprise focusing exclusively on high-value aerospace and defense programs. This has unlocked massive margin expansion and elevated the company's Return on Equity (ROE) to a world-class **48.23%**.

### 3. Deep-Dive Financial Statement Analysis

#### A. Income Statement: Stellar Top and Bottom Line Growth
*   **Revenue Growth:** Revenue has grown at a **16.3% CAGR** from 2022 ($29.14 Billion) to 2025 ($45.86 Billion). TTM Revenue stands at **$50.64 Billion**. Most recently, Q2 2026 Revenue reached **$13.35 Billion**, showing a powerful **21.1% YoY increase** compared to Q2 2025 ($11.02 Billion).
*   **Gross Margin Expansion:** Gross profit margins have expanded sequentially every year due to favorable pricing power and high-margin services:
    *   *2022:* 25.9% ($7.56B)

    *   *2025:* 31.5% ($14.44B)
    *   *Q2 2026:* 31.1% ($4.15B)
*   **Normalized Profitability:** Excluding unusual gains and restructuring charges, normalized net income has grown exponentially:
    *   *2022:* $2.64 Billion

    *   *Q2 2026 Normalized Income:* $2.44 Billion (+19.2% YoY vs. Q2 2025)

#### B. Balance Sheet & Liquidity: The "Hidden" Strength of Deferred Revenue
A surface-level analysis of GE's balance sheet shows a **Current Ratio of 0.982** and negative Working Capital of **-$732 Million** in Q2 2026, which might trigger liquidity concerns. However, a deeper look reveals this is a major competitive advantage:
*   **Deferred Revenue Cushion:** As of Q2 2026, GE carries **$18.10 Billion in Current Deferred Revenue** (customer down payments and cash prepayments for long-term engine maintenance agreements). 
*   **Adjusted Liquidity:** Deferred revenue is a *non-cash* liability that will be recognized as revenue once services are performed. Subtracting deferred revenue from current liabilities lowers actual current obligations to **$22.33 Billion**.
    *   *Adjusted Current Ratio:* **1.78** ($39.70B Current Assets / $22.33B Adjusted Current Liabilities).
*   **Debt & Leverage:** Total Debt has decreased from $26.15 Billion (2022) to **$19.16 Billion** in Q2 2026. Net Debt is a very manageable **$9.81 Billion**. With TTM FCF over $8 Billion, Net Debt/FCF is a highly conservative **~1.17x**, illustrating pristine solvency.

#### C. Cash Flow & Capital Allocation: Shareholder-First Focus
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF) Engine:** Free cash flow grew from $3.68 Billion in 2024 to **$7.26 Billion** in 2025. Summing the last four quarters reveals TTM FCF has surged to **$8.40 Billion**. Q2 2026 FCF reached **$2.86 Billion**, a spectacular **49% YoY increase** from Q2 2025 ($1.92 Billion).
*   **Aggressive Share Repurchases:** GE is returning massive amounts of capital to shareholders. 
    *   *2024 Buybacks:* $5.83 Billion
    *   *2025 Buybacks:* $7.55 Billion
    *   *1H 2026 Buybacks:* $4.53 Billion
    *   This aggressive buyback program has retired **50.8 Million shares (4.67% of total shares)** in just 2.5 years, driving a persistent lift in EPS.
*   **Dividend Growth:** GE hiked its quarterly dividend payment to **$492 Million** in Q2 2026 (up from ~$381 Million in previous quarters), yielding a conservative yet growing 0.6% dividend yield with a payout ratio of only ~18%.
