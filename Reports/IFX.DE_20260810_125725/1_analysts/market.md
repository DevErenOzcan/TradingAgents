7. **boll_lb (Bollinger Lower Band):** Represents 2 standard deviations below the 20-period SMA. It defines the lower boundary of expected volatility, showing where selloffs are statistically overextended.
8. **vwma (Volume Weighted Moving Average):** Incorporates trading volume into price averages. It verifies if price trends are supported by heavy trading activity, reflecting institutional consensus.
