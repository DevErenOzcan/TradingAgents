## 2. Semiconductor Industry & AMAT Sector Analysis

### Semiconductor Equipment & WFE Landscape
Applied Materials, Inc. (`AMAT`) is the global leader in semiconductor materials engineering solutions and wafer fabrication equipment (WFE). Its equipment is central to atomic-layer deposition (ALD), chemical vapor deposition (CVD), physical vapor deposition (PVD), chemical mechanical planarization (CMP), and advanced packaging.

* **Divergence Between Operational Realities and Stock Performance:** Recent global industry news highlights that chip stocks have retreated despite fundamental strength from major end-customers (such as Samsung's record profit guidance). This reflects market concern that macroeconomic discount rates, rather than immediate operational performance, are driving price action.
* **AI Hardware & Capex Digestion:** While long-term demand for High Bandwidth Memory (HBM3e/HBM4), Gate-All-Around (GAA) nanosheet architectures, and advanced sub-2nm nodes remains structural, short-term "AI trade jitters" have prompted temporary digestion phases and profit-taking in semiconductor stocks following multi-quarter rallies.
* **Geopolitical & Export Policy Factor:** Equipment vendors remain sensitive to U.S. technology export controls targeting leading-edge equipment sales to Chinese foundries. While demand for legacy node capacity in China provided strong revenue buffers over the past two years, equipment deliveries are shifting back toward tier-1 leading-edge chipmakers in Taiwan, Korea, and North America.
