### 2. Company Profile & Business Overview
Applied Materials, Inc. is the world’s largest provider of semiconductor manufacturing equipment, services, and software. Its solutions enable the fabrication of advanced logic, memory, and specialized semiconductor devices, as well as display technologies. 

**Core Operating Segments:**
1. **Semiconductor Systems:** Materials engineering solutions for wafer fabrication including Deposition (CVD, PVD, ALD), Chemical Mechanical Planarization (CMP), Etch, Ion Implantation, Inspection, and Patterning.
2. **Applied Global Services (AGS):** High-margin services, spare parts, software, and tools maintenance that optimize fab yield and uptime, providing recurring cash flows.
3. **Display and Adjacent Markets:** Products for manufacturing flat panel displays, flexible OLEDs, and consumer electronics.

### 7. Actionable Trading & Investment Insights
1. **Growth at a Reasonable Price (GARP):** With a forward P/E of 24.19x and a PEG ratio of 0.98x alongside projected EPS expansion to $18.47, AMAT is favorably priced relative to its semiconductor capital equipment peers.
2. **Expanding Margins Signal Pricing Power:** Gross margin expansion above 50% coupled with operating margins reaching 33.74% indicates strong demand for AMAT's premium materials engineering tools required for advanced node manufacturing.
3. **Secular AI & Advanced Packaging Drivers:** The transition to GAA (Gate-All-Around) architectures, backside power delivery, and HBM advanced packaging requires significantly higher intensity of materials deposition and removal, directly benefiting AMAT's market share.
4. **Risk Management Focus:** Key operational risks include geopolitical export restrictions to China, macroeconomic fluctuations in consumer chip demand, and customer concentration (TSMC, Samsung, Intel, Micron).
