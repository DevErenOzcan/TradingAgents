## 1. Executive Summary
Century Aluminum Company (`CENX`) represents a highly compelling growth and value opportunity in the basic materials sector. Over the past several quarters, and specifically leading into 2026, the company has undergone a dramatic financial transformation. 

A strategic divestment in early 2026 brought in **$200.0 million in cash** and unlocked a **$287.9 million book gain**, significantly boosting liquidity, lowering net debt, and expanding stockholders' equity to $1.15 billion. Crucially, even when stripping out this large one-time gain, the core operations show powerful positive momentum. Core gross margins have expanded from **9.0% to 18.3%** year-over-year, and normalized quarterly net income has more than doubled to **$83.3 million**.

With a TTM PE of 13.42, a forward PE of **4.32**, and an incredibly low PEG ratio of **0.06**, the market is severely underpricing `CENX`'s earnings power and its structural shift toward higher-margin, cleaner aluminum operations. Consequently, a **BUY** recommendation is strongly proposed.

## 2. Company Profile & Business Dynamics
Century Aluminum Company is a primary producer of standard-grade and value-added primary aluminum, with operating smelters in the United States (including Mt. Holly, South Carolina, and Sebree, Kentucky) and Iceland (Grundartangi). 

### Key Business Drivers:
1. **The Green Energy Transition & Clean Aluminum:** Aluminum is vital for lightweighting electric vehicles (EVs), aerospace applications, and sustainable packaging. Century's Iceland smelter runs entirely on renewable hydroelectric and geothermal energy, positioning it to capture high-premium demand for "green" low-carbon aluminum.
2. **Portfolio Optimization:** The Q1 2026 divestment of a business unit for $200 million suggests a strategic pruning of non-core or higher-cost assets to focus capital on more profitable operations.
3. **High Volatility (Beta of 1.998):** As a commodity producer, CENX is highly sensitive to the London Metal Exchange (LME) aluminum prices and energy costs. The high beta indicates that CENX will amplify broader market movements, making timing and fundamental strength key.

## 3. Financial Performance Analysis

### A. Core Profitability and Revenue Trends
* **Revenue Expansion:** Revenue reached **$649.2 million** in Q1 2026, putting the company on pace for a ~$2.60 billion annualized rate, up from $2.53 billion in FY 2025 and $2.22 billion in FY 2024.
* **Phenomenal Margin Expansion:** 
  * **Gross Profit** surged to **$118.8 million** in Q1 2026 compared to $57.3 million in Q1 2025. This represents a near-doubling of the **Gross Margin from 9.04% to 18.30%**.
  * **Operating Income** grew to **$86.1 million** in Q1 2026 (13.26% operating margin), up from $42.8 million (6.75% operating margin) in Q1 2025.
* **Normalized Net Income Growth:** Stripping out unusual items, Normalized Income was **$83.3 million** in Q1 2026. This represents a monumental increase from $34.7 million in Q1 2025 and indicates a normalized annual run-rate exceeding **$330 million** (well above FY 2025's full-year normalized income of $122.7 million).

### B. One-Time Events and GAAP Net Income
In Q1 2026, CENX reported a GAAP Net Income of **$337.5 million** (diluted EPS of $3.23), heavily boosted by a **$287.9 million Gain on Sale of Business**. This transaction has fundamentally reshaped the company's balance sheet, transferring non-core assets into a massive cash war chest.

## 4. Balance Sheet Strength and Solvency

CENX’s balance sheet has transitioned from highly leveraged and asset-heavy to exceptionally liquid and robust.

* **Cash and Restricted Cash:** Cash and Cash Equivalents rose to **$244.1 million** in Q1 2026 (up from $134.2 million in Q4 2025), alongside **$89.5 million in Restricted Cash**. This provides a total cash cushion of over **$333 million**.
* **Working Capital:** Working capital surged to **$709.8 million** as of March 31, 2026 (up from $507.7 million at year-end 2025), highlighting superb short-term liquidity.
* **Debt Profile:** Total debt remains stable at **$545.9 million**. However, due to the cash surge, **Net Debt fell sharply to $301.8 million** (down from $414.1 million at year-end 2025).
* **Solvency Ratios:** 
  * **Current Ratio:** A highly robust **2.303**, ensuring excellent short-term safety.
  * **Debt-to-Equity:** An extremely manageable **42.5%** (based on Stockholders' Equity of $1.15 billion).
* **Equity Accumulation:** Stockholders' equity has expanded rapidly due to retained earnings improvement, climbing from **$355.6 million** in 2023, to **$805.6 million** in 2025, and now **$1.151 billion** in Q1 2026.

## 6. Valuation & Investment Thesis

The current market valuation of CENX does not reflect its structural improvements.

1. **Undervalued Forward Earnings:** With a share price around **$47.11**, the **Forward PE of 4.32** implies a forward EPS of **$10.91** (compared to TTM EPS of $3.51). This represents an expected earnings growth rate of over 200%.
2. **Incredibly Low PEG Ratio:** The PEG ratio sits at **0.06**. Typically, a PEG ratio below 1.0 indicates undervaluation; a PEG of 0.06 indicates a profound mismatch between the company's earnings growth rate and its valuation multiple.
3. **Price to Book (P/B):** The P/B ratio of **4.05** is reasonable given the high **Return on Equity (ROE) of 32.26%**, which demonstrates that the company is highly efficient at generating profits from its net assets.

## 7. Key Risk Factors
* **Commodity Price Risk:** Volatility in global LME aluminum prices remains the primary risk factor. A sharp drop in global demand or an oversupply of Chinese aluminum could compress gross margins.
* **Energy Costs:** Smelting is highly energy-intensive. While the Icelandic operations are protected by long-term renewable contracts, the US smelters are exposed to domestic utility grid pricing and fossil fuel volatility.
* **High Beta (1.998):** Investors should expect high price volatility in the short-term, which requires a medium-to-long-term investment horizon.
