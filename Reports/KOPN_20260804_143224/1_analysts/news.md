### 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### 3. KOPIN CORPORATION (KOPN) - STRATEGIC DEEP DIVE

#### A. The FPV Drone Defense Opportunity (The Sentinel Headset)
On June 29, 2026, **KOPN** announced it secured multiple new contracts and orders from customers evaluating its **Sentinel First Person Viewer (FPV)** headset. 
*   **Military Application:** The Sentinel headset serves as a core component for control systems guiding the US Government's one-way attack drones.
*   **Market Position:** Kopin's microdisplay and optical capabilities give it a unique moat in high-resolution, low-latency, and low-power rugged head-mounted systems. This segment represents a high-margin, recurring revenue pipeline as drone warfare programs scale.

#### B. Engineering and Manufacturing Expansion (Dallas, TX)
On June 23, 2026, **KOPN** announced plans to establish a brand-new **Optics and Photonics Design Center** in Dallas, Texas, slated to open by the end of 2026.
*   **Purpose:** This facility will focus on advanced research, customized design, and small-scale manufacturing.
*   **Strategic Advantage:** Expanding its US engineering footprint allows the company to cater more effectively to Defense Advanced Research Projects (DARPA) and Department of Defense (DoD) clients who require high security and strict domestic compliance.

#### C. Financial Transformation & Guidance
Over the past three years, led by CEO Michael Murray, **KOPN** has restructured its operational model. 
*   **Revenue Guidance:** The company has reiterated its **FY26 revenue outlook of $56 million**. 
*   **Government Dependency Recovery:** While Q4 2025 results were heavily impacted by US government shutdown delays and milestone recognition pauses, the current pipeline of commercialized products (such as Sentinel) aims to normalize cash flows and insulate the business from standard bureaucratic bottlenecks.
