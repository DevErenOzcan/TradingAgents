### 4. Balance Sheet Strength & Liquid Assets Analysis
`TSM` owns a fortress balance sheet that virtually eliminates any solvency or default risk, giving it a profound advantage in a highly capital-intensive industry.

#### The Price-to-Book Discrepancy (A Key Trader Arbitrage)
Many automated screening tools list `TSM`’s P/B ratio at **97.33x** based on a reported Book Value of **$4.86 per share**. This is a mathematical error caused by dividing total equity by the total outstanding ordinary shares in Taiwan but matching it against the US-traded ADR price (which has a 1:5 ratio).
* **Actual Equity Calculation (Q2 2026):** Common Stock Equity is **6,432.52 billion TWD (~$206.83 billion USD)**. 
* Since 1 US ADR represents 5 ordinary shares, there are **5.186 billion ADR equivalents**.
* **Actual Book Value per ADR:** 6.43252 trillion TWD / 5.1865 billion ADRs = 1,240.25 TWD = **$39.93 USD per ADR**.
* **Actual Price-to-Book Ratio:** $472.78 / $39.93 = **11.84x**. For a company generating a **39.97% Return on Equity (ROE)** and a **19.00% Return on Assets (ROA)**, an 11.8x P/B is highly attractive and indicates that the stock is far more reasonably valued than automated screens suggest.

#### Outstanding Cash Cushion
* **Cash & Short-Term Investments (Q2 2026):** **3,597.44 billion TWD (~$115.67 billion USD)**, comprising 3.134 trillion TWD in cash and 463.22 billion TWD in highly liquid short-term investments.
* **Total Debt:** Only **1,064.95 billion TWD (~$34.24 billion USD)**.
* **Net Cash Position:** **2,532.49 billion TWD (~$81.43 billion USD)**. This cash position exceeds the annual GDP of several nations and ensures that `TSM` can self-fund all advanced clean room expansions without relying on restrictive debt markets.
* **Liquidity Ratios:** The current ratio stands at a robust **2.46x**, and the Debt-to-Equity ratio is extremely low at **16.56%**.
